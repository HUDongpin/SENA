## Gap Statement After Access Audit

The literature evidence supports a calibrated gap:

1. SENS/iSENS already justify combining social and epistemic network perspectives in collaborative learning.
2. ENA literature already formalizes code-code co-occurrence, matrix representations, metric-space projection, and node positioning.
3. Multilayer and heterogeneous-network literature already legitimizes block/supra-adjacency matrices and typed nodes/links.
4. The SENA gap is not "nobody has used matrices" or "nobody has combined SNA and ENA." The defensible gap is the lack of a SENA-specific formalization that treats persons and epistemic codes as different node types inside one normalized, evidence-traceable person-person / code-code / person-code supra-adjacency object.
